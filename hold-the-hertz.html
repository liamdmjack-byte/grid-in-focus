CONTROL ROOM BRIEFING

The one rule of frequency
Frequency moves at a rate of imbalance ÷ inertia. If generation exceeds demand, the needle rises; if demand exceeds generation, it falls. A 1,000 MW shortfall moves the needle twice as fast when inertia has halved — that's why the late shift is twitchy, and why the real GB grid gets harder to balance as spinning turbines retire.
⛽ Gas peaker — slow but mighty
Ramps at ~130 MW/s toward your setpoint, so a 600 MW request takes ~5 seconds to arrive. Real thermal plant is slower still — minutes, not seconds — which is why operators dispatch it ahead of need. The moment a big ticket warns you, start the gas ramping. Don't forget to ramp it back down before the event expires, or you'll overshoot high.
🔋 Battery — instant, but a sprinter
Responds the same tick you press the button, like real grid batteries delivering Dynamic Containment in under a second. Its job is to catch the first swing while slower plant catches up — then back it off and let gas carry the load. It holds ~40 seconds at full output, so recharge (run it negative) in calm spells. It's also one of only two tools that can pull frequency down by absorbing power.
💧 Pumped hydro — fast both ways
Ramps ~320 MW/s and swings between generating (+) and pumping (−), like Dinorwig reaching full output in tens of seconds. It's your best answer to surplus events — storm fronts and solar surges — because pumping soaks up excess energy and refills the reservoir at the same time. Generating drains it, so don't lean on it all shift.
🏠 Demand flex — the emergency 500
Removing 500 MW of demand has exactly the same effect on frequency as adding 500 MW of generation — and it lands instantly. The catch: 8 seconds, then a cooldown. Save it for the moment a TV pickup stacks on top of a wind lull. (Real-world cousin: the Demand Flexibility Service that paid households to back off at peak.)
The pattern that wins
Ticket warns → battery/hydro instantly catch the swing → gas ramps in behind → battery backs off to recharge → before the ticket expires, unwind everything, because the event's megawatts vanish in one tick and the needle will fly the other way.
