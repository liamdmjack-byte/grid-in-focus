Who runs your bit of the grid?

Britain's electricity distribution network is divided between six groups, serving 14 licensed areas. They own the wires from the transmission network to your meter — and under RIIO-ED2 they're being held accountable for more than just keeping the lights on.

The signal in the noise
RIIO-ED2 is the first price control where distribution operators are explicitly rewarded for enabling flexibility — not just for keeping the lights on. All six groups earned positive DSO incentive payments in 2024-25, totalling £39.6m.
