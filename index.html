A LinkedIn series · Updated weekly

Energy networks data,
decoded.

Britain's energy system publishes a colossal amount of data. Most of it sits in PDFs, CSVs, and dashboards that assume you already know what you're looking for. This series turns that raw output into something you can read in two minutes.

— What this is

One question. One dataset. One chart that earns its place. Every dashboard pulls live from the source — NESO, Elexon, Ofgem, the DNOs — so the numbers move when the grid does.

Built for the curious non-specialist as much as the practitioner.

i.
Live, not static
Every dashboard fetches data on page load. No screenshots, no stale numbers, no "as of" footnotes that age badly.
ii.
Source over speculation
Public data only — NESO Data Portal, Elexon BMRS, Ofgem, ENA Open Networks. Methodology stated, links provided.
iii.
One insight per post
No dashboards-of-dashboards. Each post answers a single question with the visual that best earns its space.
— About the series

Why I'm doing this.

I work in the UK energy sector and spend a lot of my time helping colleagues navigate the same datasets — NESO publications, ENA portals, Ofgem reports — that I'm going to surface here. The questions they ask are usually variations of "what does the data actually say?", and the honest answer is "it takes a while to find out."

This series is the version of those answers I wish existed. Built in the open, live-wired to source data, designed to be readable in the time it takes to drink half a coffee.

Each post lands on LinkedIn first. The full interactive dashboards live here.

The Grid in Focus is an independent project. All datasets are publicly available and are credited on each dashboard. Views are personal and don't represent any employer.
