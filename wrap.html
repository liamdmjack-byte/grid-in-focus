The quiet headline of 2026
The loudest stories were the auction, the deadlines and the storms. The quiet one was structural: this was the year demand stopped falling and turned — the data centres of № 14, the furnaces of № 25, the heat pumps and chargers of № 7 finally outweighing decades of efficiency gains. The grid spent forty years managing decline. It now plans for growth, indefinitely. Almost nobody noticed the turn. It was the biggest story of the year.
